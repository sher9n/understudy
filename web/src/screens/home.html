How Understudy optimizes your models

Connect once. Understudy continuously tests, learns and improves routing.

1Understand your workloads
Understudy groups similar calls by the job they’re doing.
2Find better model options
Alternative models are tested on your real calls against the quality bar set by your current model.
3Learn from real outcomes
Retries, failures, corrections and outcomes show which models actually work in production.
4Optimize routing
When another model proves better for a workload, Understudy can shift traffic gradually, and keep re-testing as things change.
