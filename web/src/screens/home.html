How it works

Four steps, and after the first they run on their own.

1Connect your traffic
Route calls through us or send us request and response pairs. We automatically group your calls by the job they are doing.
2Test cheaper ways on your calls
We replay a sample of your real calls on cheaper models, and on a cheaper model whose answers are checked, and hold each one to the bar your own model sets.
3Learn from what happens next
A call sent again, a tool that failed, a person correcting it or a result you report: every call tells us whether its answer worked.
4Keep improving
When a cheaper way is proven on your live calls, we switch to it. If one starts working less well than your own model, we switch back.
Checked cheaper models

Cheap where it can be. Yours where it has to be.

Most cheaper models are not wrong on every call, only on some. So a cheaper model answers first, a quick check reads its answer, and when the check is unsure, your own model answers instead. You keep most of the saving, and the calls it would get wrong still get your model’s answer.

Learns from outcomes

Learns from what actually worked

A measurement proves a cheaper way gives your answers. Your live calls show whether answers worked. A few calls, within a daily budget you set, try a cheaper runner-up or your own model to compare. When the evidence is strong enough, we move, and tell you why in numbers.

Would you rather nothing changed on its own? Runners-up can answer in the background instead, and you approve.

Agents, step by step
An agent’s loop is many calls. We link them into one task, so you see every step, which model answered it and which tool failed. A failed tool counts against the call that asked for it.
Tell us what worked
Only your system knows whether a ticket was resolved. Send one request with the call’s id and what happened, and it counts for more than anything we read from the traffic.
