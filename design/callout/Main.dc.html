Quality callout THREE WAYS TO SAY IT WITHOUT THE GREEN CARD

The same curve in all three. Only the way quality gets stated changes. What is on the sign in screen now is the filled green card sitting in the top right of the plot.

AOn the points
The card goes, and every point on the line becomes a tick. Four ticks say at every step without a sentence having to, and the only words left are one quiet green line. The most confident of the three.
BA second line
Quality stops being a claim and becomes a series: one flat green line above the falling one, with both labelled. It is the literal picture of what the copy above promises, and it fills the same empty corner the card was covering.
CJust the sentence
Same words, same tick, no container. Often the card is the problem and not the copy. Lightest change of the three and the only one that keeps the wording exactly as it is.
