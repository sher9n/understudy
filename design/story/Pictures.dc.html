THE PICTURES THAT CHANGE
Four drawings the story needs and the page does not have
Drawn in the product's own line style, not illustrated. Each one carries a beat of the story that is currently carried by a sentence, or by nothing at all. The floor band, the request path and the drift chart stay exactly as they are.
REPLACES THE ACCOUNT CARD

The compliance beat, said as a feature. The old drawing showed a stranger being refused, which argues with somebody. This one just shows where the work happens and what is kept.
REPLACES STEP TWO

The most surprising thing the product does, currently drawn as six repeated runs. Nobody reading that picture learns that the jobs were discovered rather than configured.
REPLACES STEP THREE
It certifies, then you switch
Shows the order, which is the promise: enough data, inside your tolerance, certificate, and only then a button nobody has pressed.
