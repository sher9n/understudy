UNDERSTUDY / THE STORY, AND HOW WE SAY IT
The same answers, for a fraction of the spend. Measured, then certified.
This is the one page to read before writing any copy for Understudy. Everything below is either the story in the order it has to be told, or a rule about how to say it.
The story, in the order it has to land
1
Lead with the measurement, never with the price tag
We ran open weight models against the frontier reference on public data and published what happened. The result comes first because it is the only thing that earns the rest of the page.
2
Matched means matched, and the bar is the reader's own model
Run the reference twice on the same input and it disagrees with itself. That gap is the bar. A model inside it is not a compromise, it is the same work. This is the sentence that keeps the word cheap out of the whole product.
3
Compliance is a plain feature, stated positively
Bedrock and providers like it host open weights inside the EU and the US, with zero data retention and data residency. Your account, your region, nothing kept. Never framed as a defence, never a word about where any model came from.
4
The data is open, so the claim is checkable
Six openly licensed Hugging Face datasets today, and the list keeps growing. Anyone can pull the same rows and rerun it. This is the difference between a result and an advertisement.
5
Then the same measurement runs on their traffic, by itself
One line of code. The system groups their calls into the jobs they actually do, with no labelling, and keeps measuring as prices and models move.
6
Certification comes before the offer, and the switch is theirs
Enough of their data, inside their own tolerance, and only then a certificate. The button appears after the evidence, never before it, and nothing moves until they press it.
7
Fine-tunes are next, and are said as roadmap
A small model trained on one job can outperform a frontier model at a fraction of the cost. Four are already on the board. Say what is coming without claiming it has shipped.
