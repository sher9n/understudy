How it works

1Connect your traffic
Route calls through us or send us request and response pairs. We automatically group your calls by the job they are doing.
2Auto-run randomized trials
We sample your real traffic, test it across multiple models, and measure accuracy, performance, and cost against your current model.
3Continuously optimize
We keep re-testing as models, quality, and prices change, so your workloads stay on the best fit without manual testing or ongoing tuning on your end.
